The XRPL has a built-in decentralized exchange — no smart contracts, no AMM (for this module), just native order book matching.                                

You'll create an offer to trade XRP for LAB tokens, verify it on the order book, cancel it, and confirm it's gone. Everything is on the testnet, so offers here
cost nothing real and can be cleaned up at any time.                                                                                                           

Prerequisite: You must have completed Trust Lines 101 first. This module uses the LAB token and issuer from that module. If you have not completed it, run     
xrpl-lab run trust_lines_101 first.

Press Enter to continue...Create a DEX offer                                                                                                                                             

Now you will submit an OfferCreate transaction. This places an order on the XRPL's built-in order book.                                                        

Your offer says: "I will pay 10 XRP to get 50 LAB."                                                                                                            

This means someone who has LAB tokens and wants XRP can fill your offer. On testnet, it will likely sit in the order book unfilled — which is exactly what we  
want, because the next step is to verify it is there.                                                                                                          

Key concepts:                                                                                                                                                  

 • TakerPays: what the taker of your offer pays (the asset you want to receive)                                                                                
 • TakerGets: what the taker of your offer gets (the asset you are offering)                                                                                   
 • From your perspective: you GET TakerPays and you PAY TakerGets

Press Enter to continue...Cancel the offer                                                                                                                                               

Open offers tie up reserves and can be filled at any time. When you no longer want to trade at that price, you cancel. This is an OfferCancel transaction      
referencing the offer's sequence number.                                                                                                                       

Cancelling is a normal transaction that costs a fee but releases the reserve.

After cancellation, the offer should no longer appear in your active offers. This confirms the cancellation was processed by the ledger.

Press Enter to continue...What you proved                                                                                                                                                

You just completed the DEX offer lifecycle:                                                                                                                    

 1 Created an offer — placed an order on the XRPL's native order book                                                                                          
 2 Verified it was active — confirmed the offer existed on-ledger                                                                                              
 3 Cancelled the offer — removed it from the order book                                                                                                        
 4 Confirmed cancellation — verified the offer is gone                                                                                                         

Key concepts to remember:                                                                                                                                      

 • Native DEX: the XRPL order book is built into the protocol, not a smart contract                                                                            
 • TakerPays / TakerGets: confusing at first, but remember — from the taker's perspective                                                                      
 • Offer sequence: every offer gets a sequence number you use to cancel it                                                                                     
 • Reserve impact: each open offer costs 0.2 XRP reserve (released on cancel/fill)                                                                             
 • Partial fills: offers can be partially filled — the remaining amount stays on the book                                                                      
 • Self-trading: you cannot fill your own offers
